About

My research and professional interests sit right where mechanical engineering, reliability, and complex industrial systems meet. I am always open to collaborations in that space.

Projects

Estimation of Production Lead Time for 20 Pharmaceutical Products

Conducted a systematic analysis of lead times for liquid and lyophilized formulations to identify bottlenecks. Mapped every step from raw material staging to packaging, uncovering inefficiencies in equipment turnover, freeze-drying cycles, and vial sealing. Applied time-motion assessments and resource alignment strategies to streamline process flow without compromising GMP compliance.

Output up from 62% to 78%

Design, Construction, and Validation of a Vial Crusher

Developed a vial crusher specifically designed to safely dispose of glass vials containing carcinogenic solutions. Used SolidWorks for detailed 3D modeling and structural analysis, ensuring optimal containment and mechanical performance. Conducted thorough motor torque calculations across varying vial sizes, with validation through stress testing and containment verification.

Precision Cooling Heat Exchanger for Industrial Freeze Dryers

Designed and fabricated a heat exchanger engineered to maintain compressor temperatures below 15 °C in an industrial refrigeration system. Modeled in SolidWorks with detailed thermal analysis and cooling capacity calculations to ensure continuous precision cooling under varying load conditions, safeguarding compressor performance.

Design and Fabrication of a Vial Inspection Booth

Engineered a dedicated inspection booth aimed at enhancing the visual examination process for faulty vials within the production facility. Built with optimized lighting and ergonomic features, the system enabled meticulous scrutiny of vial defects such as cracks, leaks, and discolorations.

Detection efficacy up from 60% to 75%

Vertical Car Parking System with Forklift Mechanism

Undergraduate thesis: Designed and constructed a 1:20-scale parking system using a forklift mechanism and ultrasonic slot detection. Included CAD-based dimensioning, mass/torque calculations, and a physical model integrating Arduino-controlled IR sensors to optimize space and indicate available slots.

Paper Cutting Machine using Geneva Mechanism

Developed a compact paper-cutting machine that uses a Geneva mechanism to convert continuous motor rotation into precise intermittent indexing of paper sheets. The wheel reliably positions each sheet at the cutting station, ensuring uniform, repeatable trims with minimal waste.
